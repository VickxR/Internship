About REC Hacks

REC Hacks is a flagship hackathon initiative organized by Rajalakshmi Engineering College (REC), Chennai, with the vision of fostering innovation, creativity, and technical excellence among students.

About Rajalakshmi Engineering College

Established in the year 1997, Rajalakshmi Engineering College is an autonomous institution affiliated to Anna University, Chennai.

REC offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs and is accredited by NBA and NAAC.

Why REC Hacks?

REC Hacks provides a platform where students collaborate, ideate, and develop innovative real-world solutions.

Our Vision

To cultivate innovation, curiosity, and collaborative learning while preparing students for global challenges.
